CTT Catalyst · delivered01
Why we built CTT Catalyst

Turning trial data into answers still takes a lot of manual work

The clinical-operations data is already unified on NextGen. But turning it into an answer for a study leader still means pulling numbers together by hand, which is slow, easy to get inconsistent, and hard to rely on when a decision is due.

Scattered signals

Enrollment, screen-failure, milestones and site risk sit in different places — no single trial-level view.

Manual & slow

Every status read is re-assembled by hand in spreadsheets — days of effort, repeated per study.

Numbers disagree

Different people, different definitions — the same metric doesn't always agree.

No narrative or actions

Raw data, but no on-demand report that ranks risk and recommends next actions.

The opportunity

Turn the already-unified data into instant answers, live dashboards and written trial reports — one trusted source, on demand, scaling from one study to the whole portfolio.

What sits behind the gap to 100%

By design: 4 very complex, multi-part questions are kept in and still show some issues — routed to the built-in risk engine that answers them directly and reliably. Data-limited: a few checks are constrained by missing source data (see next slide) — an upstream data gap, not a platform limit.

Transparency · data completeness

The reports are written summaries, and they include a gap analysis

The written trial reports read as plain-language summaries. Since not all the source data is in the system yet, we chose to be clear about the gaps rather than work around them quietly. Each report includes a dedicated gap analysis that documents every limitation with its impact and a mitigation. These are upstream data issues, not platform limits.

Flagged gapMitigation in place
No daily enrollment curvesPoint-in-time plan-vs-actual snapshot

study_name 98.6% NULLGraceful fallback to study_code

8 limitations (D1–D8) surfaced in a dedicated "Data Sources & Known Gaps" section of every report.

Why it doesn't undermine the delivery

All 29 requirements are 100% addressable and 0 are blocked by missing data. The gaps limit certain over-time questions — the platform, the definitions and the reports all work as built.

The GAP analysis is a deliverable

Every gap ships with impact + mitigation inside the report — so leaders trust the numbers and know exactly what's missing. Closing the gaps is upstream data enrichment, planned for Phase 2.
